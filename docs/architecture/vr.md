# VR output (OpenXR)

Detail behind the VR bullet in `src/CLAUDE.md`. Entries marked ADDED compile and were driven in code; none was tried on the headset yet.

- ✅ **VR output (OpenXR)** - View > "Show in VR Headset" (`MoleculeViewer::setVrActive`, `vrsession.*`, `QURCUMA_HAS_XR`; WiVRn + PICO 4 Ultra, `adb reverse tcp:9757 tcp:9757`). `viewerxr.qml` = XrView, head/hands, pick/rotate, trigger-grab (ADDED); `VrScene.qml` + `VrPanel*.qml` = molecule and panels laid out around the tracked head, no context properties, previewed offline by `spikes/vr_preview`.
- **ADDED (VR usability, not yet tried on the headset)**: both hands identical (`Hand` in `viewerxr.qml` drives `VrButton` hover/click itself via `VrPanelHost::rayHit`; no `XrVirtualMouse`: Qt 6.11 picks XrItems as infinite planes, so nearer/hidden panels swallowed clicks), see-through `VrMenuBar.qml` toggles panels + `VrPanelHelp.qml` (open at start), B / Y = hide/show panels.
- **ADDED (VR locomotion + surroundings, not yet tried on the headset)**: left stick walks / right stick snap-turns 45° by moving the `XrOrigin` (`viewerxr.qml`); View ▸ VR Surroundings = panorama sky box (`lightProbe`, also lights the scene) + runtime-loaded 3D scene (`RuntimeLoader` in `VrScene.qml`, world-fixed at the floor, metres or cm), persisted in `Settings::vr*`.
- **ADDED (VR, not yet tried on the headset)**: the headset draws the desktop `SceneController` itself: its 12 instance tables reach the XR window as `MirrorInstancing` (`tableinstancing.*`, `xrscenebridge.*`; Qt refuses one instancing object in two windows), `MoleculeModels.qml` is shared with `viewer3d.qml`, rotation stays separate per side, ray picks map rows via `atomIndexForInstance` (`test_instancing_mirror`). Toggled Models are `ToggledModel`: Qt 6.11 draws a Model hidden at first sync without its parents' transform. **ADD**: framerate measurement.
- **ADDED (VR hand input, not yet tried on the headset)**: `vr.handInput` (View ▸ VR Input, View panel; `Settings::vrHandInput`) swaps the controller `Hand`s for `VrHandInput.qml`: pinch from joints 5/10 (no runtime pinch action needed), touch only, no rays: a pinch acts on what it touches at its start (`VrPanelHost::touchHit`, `SceneController::atomAtPoint`), else nothing; in a simulation a touched atom is pulled and the molecule never moves, otherwise hold/carry (`VrScene.moleculeOffset`) + two-hand resize; B / Y returns to controllers. Hand tracking confirmed on the headset.
- **ADDED (VR usability II, not yet tried on the headset)**: selected atoms get a pulsing shell (`SceneController::selectionHaloInstancing`, 13th mirrored table, drawn only in `VrScene.qml`, toggled on `selectionCount` against the Qt first-sync transform bug); the atom under a ray or fingertip a bright shell (`VrScene.setHighlight`, plain Models in the atom node sized by `atomDrawnRadius`); min/max fields open `VrNumberPad.qml`; `VrTemperatureSlider.qml` = the dock's thermostat slider (linear, shares its min/max via `simTempRange`/`simTemp` commands); every panel has a handle (`VrPanelHost`: `moveCentreTo` → yaw/pitch/distance, Recenter resets). Drag targets (`vrDragTarget` + `vrDrag*`) are driven by rays and pinches.
- **ADDED (VR multi-grab + panel spacing, not yet tried on the headset)**: grab forces are per source (`SimulationWorker::injectForce(source, …)`, summed in `currentInjectedForces`; 0 = mouse `kMouseGrabSource`, 1/2 = `VrSession::Right/LeftGrabSource`), so both hands/controllers (and the mouse) pull at once, same atom or two. Console panel yaws from their widths (`VrScene.besideYaw`, `Style.panelGapDegrees`), hands mode pitch -32 (`consolePitch`).
- **ADDED (VR carry + system temperature, not yet tried on the headset)**: no simulation: controller trigger on the molecule (atom hit, or ray within half the fitted radius of its centre) carries it at the grab distance with its offset kept, grip rotates (also while carrying); hand pinch within `Style.moleculeTouch` (4 cm) of an atom holds it (moves + turns with the palm). `VrScene.moveMoleculeTo` shared. Thermostat slider shows the instantaneous MD temperature (`VrSession::systemTemperature`, ~4 Hz from `frameReady`) as a notch + "system" line.
- **ADDED (VR panels III, not yet tried on the headset)**: menu bar order Simulation · Structure · View (as the panels stand); file browser is a child of Structure (`VrPanelHost.anchorHost`: place = offset from the anchor, follows its moves, hidden with it); `VrButton.selected` lights the running MD/Opt (`VrSession::simulationMode`) and the chosen method also while disabled; Structure panel names the molecule (`VrSession::structureFile/structureTitle`, title from XYZ comment / PDB TITLE / MOL2 name, `structureTitleFromFile` in mainwindow.cpp).
- **ADDED (VR demo mode, not yet tried on the headset)**: View ▸ VR Demo Mode (`MainWindow::m_vrDemoMode`, session only) skips the modal "Unsaved changes" prompt in `loadMoleculeFile` and discards the modified structure; switched on whenever a VR session starts (`vrActiveChanged`).
